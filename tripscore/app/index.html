TripScore

Explainable driving-risk score from dashcam video, built on the VAST video stack: YOLO11 boxes → tracked time-to-collision → reviewed by two vision models for fault → evidence clips.

Ask about the fleet

Relative risk index across these Toronto trips, not an actuarial model. Premium figures are illustrative. Only events where the insured driver is at fault count; pedestrians crossing lawfully and other drivers' mistakes are not penalised, and good yielding earns credit. Scores describe a trip, not a person. Every flagged event links to the clip that caused it.
