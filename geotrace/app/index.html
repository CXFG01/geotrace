How to read this — and limits

City-scale (downtown Toronto road network), not world-scale. Turn sequences alone leave hundreds of candidate places; the text anchors are what makes the match unique. Held-out checks measure self-consistency, not GPS accuracy: there is no GPS ground truth in this dataset. Turn directions are not used for matching: a numpy optical-flow check confirmed 48 of the model's 87 labelled turns (7 contradicted), but the route between signs is too coarse to validate turn by turn. Model misreads (for example a street name seen on a distant sign) are rejected when they cannot fit the distance and time constraints. The same technique can reveal where footage was recorded, so this demo runs only on the provided dataset and is framed as claim verification.
